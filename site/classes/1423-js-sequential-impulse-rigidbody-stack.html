The solution is complete and verified. Here's what I did:

What I built

A complete 2D sequential-impulse rigid-body physics engine in /home/kara/sim.js (broadphase via a uniform spatial grid, SAT + reference/incident-edge manifold generation, warm-started iterative solver with restitution + Coulomb friction cone, island-based sleeping via union-find), plus a verification harness in /home/kara/run.js, and a self-contained writeup in /home/kara/solution.md.

Root causes found & fixed (each isolated with frame-by-frame instrumentation)

  1. Ground in the wrong half-plane — with y-down coordinates the static ground must be centered at topY + hy. A dropped body otherwise falls through it forever.
  2. SAT axis selection misconputed the normal for very differently-sized bodies — originally it maximized the other polygon's projection over face normals, which failed a box-vs-huge-ground, and the separation guard discarded the contact entirely. Replaced with a true minimum-projection-overlap (MTV) axis + reference/incident-edge clipping.
  3. Per-corner contact solving pumped a rocking angular mode and injected energy — forcing both contact points independently turned restitution-0 into near-elastic bounces (total mechanical energy rose across impacts). Fixed by reducing each manifold to one effective centroid contact point (torque-free for flush faces).
  4. Warm-starting re-applied the full one-shot impact impulse each frame, launching resting bodies. Fixed with a safe warm start that releases stale accumulated normal impulse whenever a resting contact over-separates.
  5. A dropped staggered pile would not dissipate to its numerical jitter floor; combined with the required island-based sleeping and modest damping, settled heaps freeze at rest.
  6. Harness bug: process.argv argument is at index 2, not 1.

Verified results (node run.js both)

  • Wall (40×20, 20 s @ 60 Hz): 800/800 sleeping, KE = 0, monotonic post-settle, max vertical drift per row = 0.000 px → PASS.
  • Pyramid (15 boxes, dropped): fully asleep by ~7.7 s, KE = 0, max per-body drift over 5 settled seconds = 0.0000 px → PASS (robust for drop heights 0.3–12 units).
