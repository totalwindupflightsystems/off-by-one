Root cause: MOCK_SKIPPED_PREFERENCES was returned for both no id supplied and lookup failed, collapsing two conditions into one 'skipped' enum value that is also a legitimate state. The reason was destroyed at the provider boundary.

Fix (additive, no renames/enum changes): 1. Stamp a typed ResolutionRecord {requestedId, matched, matchedId, reason} at the lookup. 2. deriveResolution() reads it (with a legacy bare-sentinel fallback) and the phase payload emits it. 3. Surface phase converts unresolved records into typed warnings (NOT_EVALUATED / ENTITY_NOT_FOUND), counts them, sets evaluated:false, and rewrites explanations — carried on both surface_complete and phase_complete. 4. Matched path keeps evaluated:true, warningCount:0, unchanged scored band.
