Algorithm

Exact junction-tree message passing over the supplied decomposition. For bag B_u, DP_u[assignment] = min subtree cost, charging each job's tardiness at its unique forget node and each constraint at a canonical bag; children merged by minimizing over private vertices while agreeing on B_u ∩ B_child. Cost is O(m · H^(tw+1)) with H = sum(p).
