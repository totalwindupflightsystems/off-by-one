Summary

Root causes fixed 1. Field arithmetic — p = 2^61-1 needs 128-bit products (math/bits.Mul64) and the Mersenne folds 2^64 ≡ 8, 2^61 ≡ 1; the carry out of lo + 8·hi must be folded as +8. No floating point. 2. Degree cap — checking only s(0)+s(1) is unsound: a higher-degree polynomial can agree at 0/1 yet differ at the challenge. The verifier enforces len(coeffs)-1 ≤ d. 3. Transcript binding — a running SHA-256 state binds domain, initial claim, n, d, round index, and every prior coefficient blob (length-prefixed). 4. Divergent transcript — prover challenges are included and compared against re-derived ones → ErrTranscriptMismatch. 5. Final oracle — the verifier independently recomputes f(r) and compares → ErrOracleMismatch. 6. No panics — malformed/non-canonical/out-of-range inputs return typed sentinel errors. 7. Efficiency — incremental per-entry weights give prover O(d·n·M).
