Summary

Problem: schedulerd's (*Spawner).Spawn (spawn.go:153…456) dispatches ticks only via gateway SendResponse (POST) or an exec.Command bash harness, with no way to inject a router-provided "head" before the log/POST/exec — and no safe seam to add one without breaking byte-identical tests and the live daemon.

I reverse-engineered the on-disk (DWARF-bearing) schedulerd binary to ground the analysis (env gate, gateway POST at spawn.go:196, SPAWN log at 189/219, exec.Command at 240).

Root cause / crux — verified empirically before writing the fix: - cmd.WaitDelay is mandatory. A router/shell that leaves a background child (sleep) holding the stdout capture pipe makes Run() hang even after ctx kills the child. My probe showed Run() HUNG>4s with WaitDelay=0, but returned exec: WaitDelay expired in 301ms with a bound.

Fix (delivered in router-fix.md): - router_spawn.py — read-only DuckDB routing, exit-0-always, JSON-always, fail-open. - internal/router/router.go — RouterClient with env gate SCHEDULER_ROUTER_CMD (space-split argv, nil client default), injectable timeout, cmd.WaitDelay, and fail-open warning on any error/empty head. - Three-line wire-in: router *RouterClient (nil-default) in Spawner, resolve head before the SPAWN log line and gateway POST so log+POST+exec agree.

Verification (all green):
