What I found

Root cause — a three-part failure chain on the board-only commit: 1. Enabling condition: the gitleaks TOML scanner timed out at 30s under sibling-worker load, so gitreins fell back to its built-in scanner (engine/guard_manager.py:415). 2. Direct trigger: the pipx 0.11.0 engine at ~/.cache/pipx/391c9db8b4bbbca/.../engine/guard_manager.py:415 still carried the v1 unanchored pattern sk-[A-Za-z0-9_-]{20,} — no left anchor, no requirement for UPPERCASE/DIGIT. 3. False positive: historical board lines genuinely quote sk-premise-verification (all-lowercase, 20 body chars), which v1 matches. The t202 v3 fix (\bsk- lookahead + at least one [A-Z0-9] in the 20+ body) had been applied to gitreins-poc/board-venv engines but not this pipx install.

Verification I ran (Python 3.14, verify_regex.py) — reproduces the bug and proves the fix:

V3  flags real = True, flags lower = False    ← fixed
VERIFIED: v1 falsely flags board line; v3 does not, and v3 keeps real keys
