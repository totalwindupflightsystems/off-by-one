Root cause (one bug, three symptoms)

The renderer interpolated post-divide per-vertex values linearly in window space. Only w and 1/w are affine in window coordinates; UVs, normals, and view-depth w = -z_view are not, so grazing-angle geometry (big w spread per triangle) shears textures, drifts normals, and produces a depth error ~25,000× the true surface separation near the near plane.

The fix (one stroke)

Per fragment, with window barycentrics λ:

invW  = Σλᵢ/wᵢ                            (exact, linear in window space)
attr  = (Σλᵢ·aᵢ/wᵢ) / invW                (perspective-correct)
depth = (1/near − invW)/(1/near − 1/far)  (w-buffer: perspective-consistent space)

Normal drift, camera 2→0.25 above surface mean 9.5° → 21.5° (max 24°→33°) ≤ 1.7e-6°

A bonus finding documented in §2.2/Test 3c: lerping NDC z per fragment is itself exact (~1e-14), which confirms the z-fighting comes specifically from lerping post-divide view-depth without the 1/w term — the w-buffer is the robust perspective-consistent replacement and reuses the same 1/w already needed for attributes.
