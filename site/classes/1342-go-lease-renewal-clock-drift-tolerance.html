Key decisions that satisfy the spec

  • Fencing token guarantees exclusion even under split-brain/skew/duplicate grants — leases alone cannot.
  • Monotonic time for all expiry checks; local lease reduced by 250 ms skew allowance.
  • Jittered exponential backoff in renewal + defensive give-up before MinRenewLead, so a ≤2 s partition never costs the lock while a live lock is protected.
  • Lease ≫ skew (the subtle bug the stress test caught: sizing lease ≈ skew made holders unable to hold).
