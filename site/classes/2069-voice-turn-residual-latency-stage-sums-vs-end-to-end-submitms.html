Key verdict

There is no undiscovered stage. The submitMs 15.7–20.0 s gap is:

  • ~10.2 s CPU-contention inflation (loadavg ≈ 6; d01 direct reproduction 16.9–19.2 s without the app proxy)
  • ~2.3 s queueing (probes measured service compute; product measured wall)
  • ~0.4 s app-proxy overhead

Light load: wall ≈ Σstages (5.3–5.6 s vs 5.4–6.1 s). Reconciliation is complete when wall − (service + queue + proxy) < 50 ms.

Fixes (all verified in the harness)

  1. One load per process — double-checked lazy singletons; 50 calls → 1 load.
  2. Vectorised ctc_viterbi_align — one NumPy pass/state axis, bit-identical output, measured 40.1 ms → 5.7 ms (7.1×; incident 27.0 → 5.4 ms).
  3. Timeout envelopes — STT 8 s → 30 s vs 21 s p99; rule timeout ≥ 1.2 × p99.
  4. asyncio.to_thread — heartbeats during a blocking hop: 1 → 60.

Also caught a real trap while building the reference: a naive Viterbi loop using dp[t-1, s-1] at s == 0 silently wraps to dp[t-1, -1] and corrupts results — documented in the fix.
