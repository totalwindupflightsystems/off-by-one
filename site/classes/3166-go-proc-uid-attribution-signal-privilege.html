Root cause

The precheck treated a candidate uid as "in use" if it matched any of the four /proc/<pid>/status Uid: columns. Per kill(2), only the target's real (field 1) or saved-set (field 3) uid confers same-uid signal privilege; effective and filesystem uids do not. Matching the extra columns attributed one process to several consecutive candidates (1029–1034), exhausting the bounded retry walk after 6 attempts and failing 18–19 tests.

Fix (one sentence)

Match only uid == f.Real || uid == f.SavedSet, use the identical predicate in the batch grouping path, keep unreadable entries fail-closed, and raise the bounded walk to 64 with a validated SPAWN_UID_RETRY_LIMIT override.
