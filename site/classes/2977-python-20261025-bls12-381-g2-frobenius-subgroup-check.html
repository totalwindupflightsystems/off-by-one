Root cause (the short version)

BLS12-381 G2 is the M-type twist y² = x³ + 4ξ with ξ = 1+u. The untwist–Frobenius–twist map is

psi(x,y) = (conj(x)·c_x, conj(y)·c_y)

and c_x,c_y are forced by the requirement that the result lands back on E':

c_y² = c_x³ = b2 / conj(b2) = (1+u)/(1-u) = u.

Since ξ^(p-1) = -u and ξ^-(p-1) = u, the only correct choice is

c_x = ξ^(-(p-1)/3),   c_y = ξ^(-(p-1)/2)

i.e. the ξ-inverse variants, not the positive exponents and not (p-2)/2. The positive-exponent convention yields -u and produces points off the curve. In the tower these are v^(1-p) (Fp6) and w^(3(1-p)) (Fp12), which the tests verify directly.

Verification highlights

  • psi(G) == [x]G for the spec generator, and psi(G) is on E'.
  • Convention table: only (ξ^-(p-1)/3, ξ^-(p-1)/2) is on-curve and equals [x]; the other three fail.
  • Fast ψ check and naive [r]P check agree on 8 random G2 points and 8 random cofactor points; all cofactor points are rejected.
  • Known 96-byte compressed generator decompresses exactly to the spec (x,y) and round-trips; byte order x.c1‖x.c0, flag bits, and lexicographic sign rule validated; bad flags/non-canonical inputs rejected.
  • Tower identities v³=ξ, w²=v, w⁶=ξ, v^(1-p)=PSI_X, w^(3(1-p))=PSI_Y, and untwist(G) on E/Fp12 all pass.
