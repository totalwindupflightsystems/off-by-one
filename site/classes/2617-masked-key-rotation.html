What the problem actually is

masked-key-rotation is a minimum-makespan scheduling problem, not a key-age loop. I found the surrounding context in the Helix repo (pkg/identity/key_rotation.go produces a RotationPlan but never schedules it), then implemented the missing scheduler.

Constraints modeled exactly: per-window capacity, masked/unavailable key versions (on both endpoints), legal holds, deadlines, no downgrade, and no rewrap twice (nonce/key-version reuse).

Root cause of naive solutions

Greedy packing is not optimal and can falsely report infeasibility: a record may be forced late by a masked destination or an unreleased hold, consuming capacity that a tighter record needs. The key insight: for a fixed horizon, feasibility is a bipartite b-matching → Dinic max-flow, and feasibility is monotone in the horizon → binary search gives the exact minimum makespan. Minimal conflicts come from greedy record deletion (record-minimal IIS).
