What it contains

Root-cause analysis - Wall-clock leases are non-monotonic: a slow holder consumes L local ns over L/(1-ρ) real ns. - Concrete counterexample: D=10s, A's clock -3s → at true t=10s both A.valid() and B.valid() are true; both ack a write; reconciliation silently drops A's acked value. - Bounded-drift window derivation: overlap_naive = L·ρ/(1-ρ); safe sizing L_loc = D(1-ρ) - 2ε, D_auth = L_loc/(1-ρ) + 2ε, giving overlap -2ε ≤ 0.

Exact fix (full runnable Go 1.26 source) - hlc.go — hybrid logical clock (Now/Observe) used for lease deadlines and ordering. - store.go — fenced replicas (token < fence → FenceError), quorum Propose, RecoverAfterHeal that installs the max fence and keeps highest-token entries. - manager.go — SafeLocalLease/SafeAuthorityLease, LeaseAuthority (monotonic tokens), HLC LeaseGuard, Leader write/read paths. - sim_test.go — deterministic skew/partition harness.

A safety subtlety I caught and fixed: a leader that observes any replica with a strictly higher fence must abort even if it could still count a bare majority of acceptors. Propose now returns FenceError on any higher-fence observation, with TestFenceAbortOnAnyHigherFence covering it.
