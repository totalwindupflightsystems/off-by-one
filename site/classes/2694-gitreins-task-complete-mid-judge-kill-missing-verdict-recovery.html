Diagnosis

task complete is three nontransactional steps in cmd_task_complete (cli.py 0.16.0):

  1. tm.complete() writes status: complete + completed_at to tasks.yaml — line 1716, first
  2. judge.evaluate_task() runs Tier 1 + Tier 2 — line 1721 (the slow/network phase)
  3. _persist_result() writes .gitreins/history/<date>/<hash>/verdict.json — line 1725, last

A kill between 1 and 3 leaves the store advanced with no evidence. complete is a claim; the verdict directory is the proof.

The fix (as documented)

  • Do not re-run task complete (re-enters the same foreground Tier-2 kill window, re-stamps the task).
  • Do not use --persist-verdict — it is ephemeral-only; verified it aborts with exit 2: argument --persist-verdict: not allowed without argument --ephemeral, and it writes the wrong artifact (.gitreins/verdicts/, not history).
  • Independently verify ACs + full suite, then:
  • gitreins judge TR-211 --async → detached worker, disk-backed job
  • poll gitreins judge --status job-<hex> (exit 2=running, 0=complete, 1=error) until Verdict: COMPLETE
  • confirm verdict.json's commit equals landed HEAD
  • close with boardctl update TR-211 --status complete --evidence-run-id <verdict-dir>

The doc also covers the guarded orphaned-running-job trap (CLI --status won't resume orphans; MCP judge.status does), and includes a syntax-checked drop-in recover-missing-verdict.sh.
