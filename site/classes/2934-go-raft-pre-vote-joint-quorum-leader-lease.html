Root causes identified (two were real bugs hit while building)

  1. PreVote term handling — RequestPreVote must be dispatched before the generic "higher term ⇒ step down" path, and it must never mutate the receiver's term/log. The candidate carries proposedTerm = Term+1 and only adopts it after a pre-vote quorum.
  2. PreVoteResp term comparison bug — the responder replies with its own current term, but I initially compared it to proposedTerm, so every grant was discarded and no candidate ever formed. Fixed to compare against the candidate's unchanged n.Term.
  3. Leader-stickiness sentinel bug — lastLeaderContact initialised to 0 made every voter compute "leader is active" at tick 10 and refuse all PreVotes. Fixed to -1<<30.
  4. Joint consensus — commit quorum must be the union rule: while any config entry is uncommitted, commitMembership() returns the joint config. This makes the joint entry itself and the final C_new entry both commit under both majorities.
  5. Lease — expired in applyCommitted before logging apply_joint_config; hasLeaseQuorum is false whenever Config.Joint(), so no lease is granted before C_new commits. Also fixed the lease never being renewed (it flapped every leaseDuration); it now refreshes silently while a quorum of fresh acks exists.
  6. Determinism — sorted node slices instead of map ranges, private seeded RNG consumed only in send, pure electionTimeout(node,term), and (DeliverAt, seq) queue ordering.
